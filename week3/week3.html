About the school

Baccuit National High School is a public secondary school in Baccuit Sur, Bauang, La Union. It belongs to the Schools Division of La Union, Bauang North district, in Region I (Ilocos Region).

  • 652 learners
  • 25 teachers
  • 6 non-teaching staff
  • 14 classrooms

Programs

Junior High School (Grades 7 to 10) and Senior High School (Grades 11 to 12). The Technical-Vocational-Livelihood (TVL) track is recorded in the school year 2024–2025 data, with 134 Senior High School learners.
